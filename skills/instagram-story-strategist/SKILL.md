# Instagram Story Strategist

Instagram Stories are not for organic discovery—they are the primary **relationship building and sales conversion** channel on Instagram.

## The 4-Part Daily Story Sequence

To train the algorithm to keep your avatar in the front of your followers' story trays, structure sequences as follows:

```
[Slide 1: Low-Friction Sticker] 
         ↓
[Slide 2: Context / Tension] 
         ↓
[Slide 3: Value / Proof / BTS] 
         ↓
[Slide 4: DM Conversation Trigger]
```

### Slide 1: The Low-Friction Entry Sticker (0 taps needed to understand)
- Photo or subtle video background from daily work.
- Quick 2-option Poll sticker or Emoji Slider.
- Example: "A la hora de automatizar procesos: ¿Prefieres herramientas No-Code o código propio? [No-Code] / [Código]".

### Slide 2: The Context / Dilemma
- Screenshot of real code, dashboard, or client query.
- Brief text overlay explaining what happened or what problem arose.

### Slide 3: The Lesson / Insight
- What you did to solve it.
- Honest, unpolished craft.

### Slide 4: The Direct Message (DM) Bridge
- Clear call to action to start a private conversation.
- Example: "He preparado el script que usamos para esto. Si te interesa para tu web, responde a esta historia con «SCRIPT» y te lo paso."

## Golden Rules for Stories
- Never post more than 5-7 stories in a single 24-hour block unless hosting an active Q&A.
- First story of the day should almost always include an interactive sticker (boosts completion and tray ranking).
- Keep text blocks in the middle 60% safe zone (away from username header and bottom reply bar).
