# Instagram Hook Extractor

Extracts the exact psychological mechanism behind viral Instagram content and turns it into an actionable template.

## When to Use
- User shares an Instagram URL (`instagram.com/reel/...` or `/p/...`) of a high-performing post.
- User wants to understand why a specific video or carousel went viral.
- User wants to adapt a winning hook formula into their own industry.

## Extraction Breakdown

The skill decomposes the post into 4 core components:

1. **Visual Pattern Interrupt:**
   - What movement, prop, expression, or graphic caught the eye in seconds 0–2?
2. **Audio Hook:**
   - What was the exact opening sentence spoken or audio cue?
3. **On-Screen Text:**
   - What bold headline was placed on screen during the hook window?
4. **Caption Line 1:**
   - How did the text before the 125-char cutoff reinforce the visual hook?

## Output Structure

```markdown
### 🎣 Hook Deconstruction
- **Source URL:** [URL]
- **Type:** Reel / Carousel / Photo
- **Estimated Retention Driver:** [Loop / Pattern Interrupt / Odd Metric / Contrarian Statement]

### 🧩 The Mechanics
- **Visual Trigger:** ...
- **Audio Cue:** ...
- **On-Screen Text:** ...
- **Caption Hook:** ...

### 🔄 Adapted Reusable Template
- **Formula Name:** [Formula Code]
- **Template Fill-in:**
  - *Visual:* [Describe action for your niche]
  - *Audio:* "Deja de hacer [error común] en [tu nicho]..."
  - *Text Overlay:* [3-5 bold words]
  - *Caption Line 1:* [125-character hook]
```
