# Instagram Comment Drafter

Commenting on authority accounts in your niche is the most effective organic outreach method on Instagram. This skill drafts comments that get pinned, liked, and drive qualified profile visits.

## Rules for High-Value Comments
1. **Never Self-Promote:** Do not mention your handle, website, or services. Let your insight earn the profile click.
2. **Never Post Filler:** Ban "¡Totalmente de acuerdo! 🔥", "Gran post 🙌", "100% real". These are ignored as bot spam.
3. **The 3-Sentence Structure:**
   - Sentence 1: Validate a specific detail from the post (shows you actually watched/read).
   - Sentence 2: Add one unique insight, concrete nuance, or metric from your own work.
   - Sentence 3: A brief open-ended conclusion or nod to the creator.
4. **Length:** 100 to 250 characters. Punchy, skimmable, and visually clean.

## Step-by-Step Flow
1. User provides post URL or pasted caption of the target creator.
2. Skill analyzes the core thesis.
3. Skill drafts 2 distinct options:
   - *Option A (The Nuance / Add-on):* Deepens the post's point with a technical or practical detail.
   - *Option B (The Contrarian Twist):* Politely reframes the exception to the rule.
4. Shows approval card with target URL.
