# Instagram Content Planner

Plans an Instagram publishing cadence that balances top-of-funnel reach with bottom-of-funnel client acquisition.

## The 3-Pillar Instagram Funnel

| Funnel Stage | Format | Frequency | Primary Goal | Algorithm Driver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top (Reach)** | Reels (9:16) | 2–3x / week | New eyes / Non-followers | Watch time >80%, Shares |
| **Middle (Authority)**| Carousels (4:5) | 1–2x / week | Nurture & Trust | High Saves (3-4x photos) |
| **Bottom (Sales)** | Stories + DM Trigger | Daily (3-5 slides) | 1-on-1 DM Conversations | Sticker taps & Replies |

## Standard 7-Day Plan Blueprint

- **Lunes (Reel — Top of Funnel):**
  - Angle: Pattern interrupt / Common mistake (e.g. IH1).
  - Goal: Discovery across Explore / Reels tab.
- **Miércoles (Carrusel — Middle of Funnel):**
  - Angle: Step-by-step breakdown / Framework cheat-sheet (e.g. IH7).
  - Goal: Saves and reference utility.
- **Viernes (Reel o Foto Caso Real — Bottom of Funnel):**
  - Angle: Case study / Client receipt / Before & After (e.g. IH8).
  - Goal: Inbound DMs ("Comenta «INFO»").
- **Diario (Historias):**
  - 3-5 slides with interactive polls leading to DM conversations.

## Output
Returns a markdown table for the week: Day, Format, Topic / Angle, Hook Formula, Visual Asset description, and Caption focus.
