# Instagram Profile Optimizer

Your profile bio is your landing page. You have roughly 3 seconds to answer: *Who are you, who do you help, what have you achieved, and what should I do next?*

## The 4 Pillars of Profile Conversion

### 1. Name Field (The Search Engine Anchor)
- **Constraint:** Up to 30 characters. This is the **only searchable field** on Instagram besides your @username.
- **Rule:** Do not duplicate your username. Include your core service and SEO keywords.
- *Format:* `[Nombre] | [Palabra Clave 1] [Palabra Clave 2]`
- *Example:* `Juan | Desarrollo Web & IA`

### 2. Bio Copy (150 Characters Max)
- **Line 1 (The Hook / UVP):** Who you help and what outcome you produce.
- **Line 2 (Authority / Receipts):** Real metrics, clients served, or years of craft.
- **Line 3 (The CTA):** Explicit call to action pointing to the link below.
- *Example:*
  ```
  Ayudo a negocios a automatizar tareas y vender más online ⚡
  Desarrollador web • +50 proyectos entregados
  Auditoría gratuita de tu web aquí abajo 👇
  ```

### 3. Story Highlights (Destacadas) Architecture
Organize 3–5 core highlight folders with custom branded covers:
1. **[Empieza Aquí / Quién Soy]:** Your background, values, and methodology.
2. **[Casos Reales / Resultados]:** Screenshots, before & after metrics, client audio notes.
3. **[Servicios / Cómo Trabajo]:** Clear explanation of what you offer.
4. **[Recursos / Guías]:** Free value, cheat-sheets, tutorials.

### 4. The 3 Pinned Posts (Fijados) Strategy
Keep your top 3 grid slots strategically pinned:
- **Pin 1 (Authority / Origin):** Your story, why you build what you build, your contrarian thesis.
- **Pin 2 (Proof / Case Study):** Your highest-performing client case study with undeniable receipts.
- **Pin 3 (The Flagship Lead Magnet):** A high-save carousel or Reel leading to DMs.
