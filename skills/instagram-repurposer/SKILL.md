# Instagram Repurposer

Cross-platform distribution fails when creators take a LinkedIn post or tweet and paste it unchanged as an Instagram caption. This skill translates text-heavy concepts into Instagram's native visual languages.

## 4 Repurposing Pathways

### Pathway A: Text Post → 4:5 Carousel (Best for frameworks & lists)
- **Input:** A 1,000-word LinkedIn post or 7-tweet thread.
- **Conversion:**
  - Tweet 1 / Hook → Slide 1 (High contrast headline).
  - Main points → Slides 2–6 (One clean takeaway per slide, max 35 words).
  - Conclusion → Slide 7 (Summary checklist + Save CTA).
  - Drafts a condensed 400-char Instagram caption.

### Pathway B: Text Post → 30s Reel Script (Best for contrarian takes & stories)
- **Input:** A reflective or opinion post.
- **Conversion:**
  - Extracts the core insight.
  - Translates into a 4-column script (0-3s hook, tension, fix, loop).
  - Specifies b-roll or screen recording visual actions.

### Pathway C: Long Article / Newsletter → 3-Slide Story Breakdown
- **Input:** Comprehensive article.
- **Conversion:**
  - Slide 1: Quick poll on the core problem.
  - Slide 2: The single biggest takeaway graphic.
  - Slide 3: Link sticker or DM trigger to read full piece.

### Pathway D: Quote / Insight → 4:5 Typeset Card
- **Input:** Single punchy sentence.
- **Conversion:**
  - Renders as a crisp HTML quote-card via `lib.quote_card(quote, ...)`.
  - Pairs with a 300-char micro-blog caption.
