# 10 Creator Angles for Business & Tech (Instagram Edition)

These 10 angles (CA1–CA10) are tailored for builders, founders, and specialized agencies on Instagram who want to build real authority and client pipeline rather than empty lifestyle virality.

---

### CA1: Demystify the Black Box
* **Territory:** Breaking down a complex technical topic or workflow into simple, digestible mechanics.
* **Goal:** Saves & Authority.
* **Format:** 7-slide Carousel (4:5) or 45s Reel screencast.
* **Structure:** "Todo el mundo habla de [concepto/IA/automatización], pero casi nadie enseña cómo funciona por dentro. Aquí está la arquitectura real."

### CA2: Proof Over Promises (Receipts)
* **Territory:** Demonstrating real outcomes using unedited dashboards, analytics, or client messages.
* **Goal:** Inbound DMs & Conversions.
* **Format:** Micro-case study Carousel or Reel talking-head over green screen screenshot.
* **Structure:** "3 números reales del último proyecto de [nicho]. Sin estimaciones ni proyecciones de consultor."

### CA3: The Costly Micro-Habit
* **Territory:** Highlighting a tiny inefficiency that wastes hours or leaks money every single week.
* **Goal:** Direct DM Shares to colleagues/partners.
* **Format:** 30s Reel or Single Infographic.
* **Structure:** "El pequeño error de 5 minutos en [proceso] que le cuesta a un negocio [X horas / X euros] cada mes."

### CA4: Tool vs. Judgement
* **Territory:** Contrasting the obsession with new apps/tools against the foundational business logic.
* **Goal:** Comments & Saves.
* **Format:** Contrarian Carousel with side-by-side comparisons.
* **Structure:** "Comprar [herramienta de moda] no arregla un flujo de trabajo desordenado. El criterio siempre va antes del software."

### CA5: The Counter-Intuitive Bet
* **Territory:** Defending a decision that goes against common industry dogma and explaining why it paid off.
* **Goal:** Comments & Reach.
* **Format:** Reel talking-head (Pattern interrupt).
* **Structure:** "Por qué decidimos dejar de hacer [lo que todos recomiendan] y qué pasó cuando empezamos a [enfoque propio]."

### CA6: Raw Behind-the-Scenes Build
* **Territory:** Showing the unpolished reality of development, debugging, and daily execution.
* **Goal:** Relatability & Trust.
* **Format:** Aesthetic Reel with workspace b-roll + honest voiceover.
* **Structure:** "Así se ve de verdad resolver un problema técnico a las 8 de la mañana: 3 pruebas fallidas y un cambio de línea clave."

### CA7: The One-Variable A/B Test
* **Territory:** Comparing two real approaches where only one parameter was altered.
* **Goal:** Saves & Discussion.
* **Format:** Side-by-side Carousel.
* **Structure:** "Mismo [objetivo], dos implementaciones distintas. Versión A vs Versión B: los datos de rendimiento tras 30 días."

### CA8: The Reusable Framework Cheat-Sheet
* **Territory:** A self-contained visual flowchart or decision tree that viewers can save and reference anytime.
* **Goal:** High Save Multiplier (3-4x).
* **Format:** Carousel with slide 1 as full map, slides 2-6 zooming into each node.
* **Structure:** "Mi plantilla de 4 pasos para auditar cualquier [sistema/web] antes de salir a producción."

### CA9: Reprice the True Cost
* **Territory:** Explaining why the 'free' or 'cheap' DIY solution is often drastically more expensive than doing it right.
* **Goal:** Client education & Qualifying leads.
* **Format:** Reel or Carousel breakdown.
* **Structure:** "Hacerlo 'gratis' te cuesta 15 horas al mes. Hacerlo profesional cuesta [X] y funciona en piloto automático. Las cuentas reales."

### CA10: The Unspoken Trade-off
* **Territory:** Clarifying what you have to give up to achieve a specific level of performance, speed, or simplicity.
* **Goal:** High trust & Expert reputation.
* **Format:** Thoughtful caption with high-impact single graphic.
* **Structure:** "En tecnología no existen soluciones mágicas; solo existen compromisos (trade-offs). Si quieres [beneficio], tienes que aceptar [peaje]."
