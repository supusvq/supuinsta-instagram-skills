# 2026 Instagram Engagement & Reach Benchmarks

Compiled across creator account studies, format engagement audits, and Meta distribution metrics.

## 1. Engagement Rates by Follower Band (Median)

| Account Tier | Followers | Median Engagement Rate (Total Engagements / Reach) | Average Reel View Rate (% of Followers) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | <10K | 3.8% – 5.5% | 35% – 70% |
| Micro | 10K – 50K | 2.1% – 3.2% | 18% – 35% |
| Mid-Tier | 50K – 200K | 1.4% – 2.2% | 10% – 22% |
| Macro | >200K | 0.9% – 1.6% | 5% – 14% |

*Note: For Reels reaching non-followers, reach can exceed 500% of follower count when completion rate passes 80%.*

## 3. Key Retention Benchmarks for Reels

- **3-Second Hook Retention:**
  * Top 10%: >72% viewers still watching at 0:03.
  * Average: 45–55% retention at 0:03.
  * Underperforming: <40% retention (signals immediate swipe-away).
- **Completion Rate:**
  * 15-30s Reels: >75% completion triggers second Explore surge.
  * >100% completion (replays/loops): triggers broad algorithm distribution.

## 4. Carousel Benchmarks

- **Optimal Slide Count:** 7 to 10 slides generates 1.4x higher engagement than 3-4 slides.
- **Swipe-through Completion:** >45% reaching the final slide correlates with maximum post saves.
- **Second-Chance Impressions:** Up to 22% of total carousel impressions occur on the second serve (Slide 2).
