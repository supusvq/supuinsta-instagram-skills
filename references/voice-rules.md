# Instagram Voice & Caption Formatting Rules (2026)

## 1. Pacing & Mobile Reading Flow
- **Line 1 Hook:** Maximum 125 characters. Must stand alone as a compelling reason to tap `... más`. Never waste line 1 on greetings ("Hola a todos"), date mentions, or generic questions.
- **Line Breaks:** Use double line breaks between independent thoughts. Walls of text kill read-through rates on 6-inch screens.
- **Pacing:** Alternate between punchy single-line statements and short 2-3 sentence explanatory blocks.

## 2. Punctuation & Typography
- **Quotation Marks:** Use clean quotes (« » or " ") for concepts and titles.
- **Bullet Anchors:** Use clean, subtle bullet markers (`→`, `•`, or numbered `1. 2. 3.`) rather than random decorative emojis.
- **Em Dashes:** Keep to a minimum (max 1 per post). A comma, colon, or parenthesis is cleaner on mobile.

## 3. Emoji Rules (No Emoji Vomit)
- **Limit:** 1 to 3 emojis per post maximum.
- **Purpose:** Functional visual anchors only (e.g. `👇` pointing to details, `📌` marking a note, `👀` highlighting an insight).
- **Prohibited:** Never use chains of consecutive emojis (`🚀🔥✨💪💯`), never replace words with emojis, and never put emojis in the first 5 words of Line 1.

## 4. Vocabulary (Anti-AI & Anti-Cliche)
- **Scrubbed Clichés:** "en este post te cuento", "descubre el secreto", "revoluciona tu vida", "lleva tu negocio al siguiente nivel", "game changer", "contenido de valor", "no te lo puedes perder".
- **Authentic Voice:** Speak from real first-person experience ("lo que comprobé", "en mi último proyecto", "la prueba que hicimos").

## 5. Length & Formatting by Asset Type
- **Reel Caption:** 150 to 500 characters. The video carries the primary weight; caption provides the reference summary and SEO context.
- **Carousel Caption:** 400 to 800 characters. Supplements the slides, reinforces the core lesson, and provides a clear Save/Share CTA.
- **Single Image / Infographic Caption:** 500 to 1,000 characters (micro-blog format).

## 6. Call to Action (CTA) & Keyword Triggers
- Single clear CTA per post:
  - For Reach: "Envíale este vídeo a [socio/amigo] que necesita verlo."
  - For Saves: "Guarda este post para cuando configures tu [sistema]."
  - For Conversion/Leads: "Comenta «AUDITORIA» y te paso el enlace por privado."
- Avoid generic engagement bait ("¿Estás de acuerdo? Comenta sí o no").

## 7. Hashtags
- Exactly 3 to 5 hyper-targeted niche hashtags.
- Placed at the very bottom, preceded by 1-2 empty lines.
- Example: `#desarrolloweb #woocommerce #automatizacionia`
