## 1. Core Signal Hierarchy (The Multiplier Weights)

Instagram's feed, Explore, and Reels algorithms prioritize **private value and conversation** over passive consumption:

| Signal | Relative Weight | Algorithmic Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **DM Share / Send to Friend** | **5.0x – 10.0x** | Primary viral catalyst: post created real-world interpersonal connection. |
| **Save** | **3.0x – 4.0x** | High-utility reference: user intends to return (crucial for Carousels). |
| **Completion Rate (Reels)** | **3.0x – 5.0x** | Over 85% completion or re-watch loop triggers Explore distribution. |
| **Thoughtful Comment (4+ words)**| **2.0x – 2.5x** | Sparks conversation; author reply within 60 mins amplifies. |
| **Carousel Swipe-Through** | **2.0x – 3.0x** | High dwell time across multiple slides. |
| **Single Like / Heart** | **1.0x (Baseline)** | Low-friction signal; saturated and least predictive of reach. |

## 2. Format Breakdown & Screen Real Estate

Never post square (1:1) in 2026 unless aesthetically mandated.

* **Reels (9:16 — 1080x1920 px):**
  * Maximum organic reach vehicle to **non-followers** (via Reels Tab and Explore).
  * Ideal length: 15–35 seconds for punchy tutorials/takes; 45–60 seconds only if storytelling justifies retention.
* **Carousels (4:5 — 1080x1350 px):**
  * Maximum relationship and save vehicle to **existing followers and warm audiences**.
  * Occupies ~25% more mobile screen height than 1:1, preventing fast scroll-past.
  * Optimal depth: 6–10 slides.
* **Single Image / Graphic (4:5 — 1080x1350 px):**
  * Best for sharp quote-cards, relatable memes, or single high-impact data visuals.
* **Stories (9:16):**
  * 24-hour conversion and relationship funnel. Not for discoverability, but for turning followers into clients/conversations via interactive stickers and DMs.

## 3. Reels Distribution Engine

The first 3 seconds dictate 80% of a Reel's lifetime reach.

1. **The 3-Second Rule:** Viewers make the swipe decision at 1.2–1.8 seconds.
   * Need a **Visual Hook** (movement, unexpected angle, facial expression, text card) + an **Audio Hook** (first spoken word or sound effect).
2. **On-Screen Captions:** 68% of users watch without sound in public spaces. Hardcoded dynamic captions or clear text overlay in the safe zone (middle 60% of screen) are mandatory.
3. **Looping Mechanics:** Crafting the final sentence to naturally flow back into the opening sentence increases replay count, inflating completion rate past 100%.

## 4. Carousel Second-Chance Re-serve Mechanism

Carousels have a unique advantage over single photos:
* If a follower sees Slide 1 in their feed and scrolls past without interacting, Instagram frequently **re-serves the same post later that day showing Slide 2**.
* **Actionable Rule:** Slide 2 must act as a *secondary hook* or immediate value deliverable, not filler context.

## 5. Caption Structure & SEO Indexing

1. **The Fold (First 125 Characters):**
   * On mobile, only the first 1–2 lines (around 125 characters) appear before the `... more` button.
   * Never start with pleasantries ("Hola a todos!", "Espero que tengáis un buen lunes"). Start with the thesis, surprising stat, or tension point.
2. **Semantic Search over Hashtags:**
   * Instagram parses caption text into topical keywords. Natural phrasing ("automatización de citas para barberías", "trucos de desarrollo web con IA") indexes better than keyword stuffing.
3. **Format Layout:**
   * Line breaks between every 1–2 sentences.
   * Use bullet anchors (•, →) for readability.

## 6. Hashtag Reality in 2026

* **0 to 5 niche hashtags** is the sweet spot.
* Generic mega-tags (`#marketing`, `#viral`, `#fyp`, `#instagood`) dilute topical categorization and invite bot comments.
* Use 3–5 hyper-relevant tags (e.g. `#desarrolloweb`, `#woocommerceespaña`, `#automatizacionia`). Place them cleanly at the very end of the caption.

## 7. Story Engagement & DM Triggering

* Daily Story sequences should start with a low-friction interaction: a poll sticker (2 options) or an emoji slider.
* The algorithm boosts users to the front of the Stories tray for anyone who regularly taps their stickers or replies by DM.
* Use DM keyword triggers (e.g., "Responde «AUDITORIA»") to move warm prospects directly into 1-on-1 chats.

## 8. The 45-Minute Momentum Window

* The first 45–60 minutes after posting determines initial cohort testing.
* **Creator Rule:** Be active in the app for 15–30 minutes post-publishing to reply to early comments with questions, doubling the comment volume and sending positive activity signals.

---

## 9. Penalties & Suppression Triggers

* **Watermarks:** Videos with TikTok, CapCut, or rival platform watermarks get suppressed by ~70% in Reels recommendations.
* **Low Resolution / Bitrate:** Blurry videos or poorly rendered 720p footage suffer lower distribution.
* **Explicit Engagement Baiting:** Phrasing like "like if you agree", "comenta un emoji para ganar" is flagged by text moderation.
* **Deceptive Clickbait:** Audio or visual promises that don't pay off cause instant swipe-away within 2 seconds, destroying the retention score.
